Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

Welcome to SunSafeRun ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any data processed when you use our route planning, sun exposure analysis, and fitness watch synchronization services.

1. Information We Process

To provide sun-optimized route recommendations and sync workouts to your devices, we process the following types of information:

  • Geolocation & Route Data: Waypoints, GPS coordinates, and route selections you draw or generate on our map to calculate shade coverage, building height shadows, and solar angles[cite: 7].
  • Connected Account Tokens (Intervals.icu / Garmin): When you choose to connect your account via OAuth 2.0, we process secure, encrypted authentication tokens to schedule workouts directly to your connected calendar and device[cite: 7].
  • Technical Usage Data: Standard server requests, web browser types, and anonymous interaction metrics required to ensure site functionality and uptime.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use processed data strictly for the following functional purposes[cite: 7]:

  • Calculating dynamic sun positions, UV indices, and shaded running paths for your selected routes[cite: 7].
  • Pushing scheduled workouts and route structures directly to your third-party account (e.g., Intervals.icu / Garmin Connect) upon your explicit request[cite: 7].
  • Ensuring platform security, performance optimization, and bug fixing.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data or route histories to advertisers or third parties[cite: 7]. Data is transmitted securely via HTTPS solely to integrated platform partners that you explicitly connect[cite: 7]:

  • Intervals.icu API: Used to convert planned run metrics into structured workout events on your personal calendar[cite: 7].
  • Vercel (Hosting Infrastructure): Used to host our serverless functions securely in accordance with enterprise data protection standards.

4. Data Retention & Access Control

We believe in minimal data footprint:

  • Route coordinates are evaluated dynamically in real time and are not permanently tied to user profile identities.
  • You may revoke SunSafeRun's access to your connected fitness accounts at any time through your third-party account settings (e.g., Intervals.icu Authorized Apps)[cite: 7].

5. Security

We implement standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) encryption to protect all data in transit between your web browser, our servers, and integrated third-party APIs[cite: 7].

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in platform features or legal compliance standards. Any updates will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
